### 1. THE DIFFERENTIATION ARREST PARADOX IN MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS

The molecular mechanism of this failure is a pathological differentiation lock:
1. **Constitutive GPR17 Overexpression:** The uracil nucleotide / cysteinyl-leukotriene receptor GPR17 acts as an intrinsic "timer" or brake on oligodendrocyte maturation. 2. **Pathological Lock:** In chronic MS perivascular inflammatory niches, extracellular damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs) and microglial cytokines trap OPCs in a permanent state of high GPR17 expression, blocking protein kinase A (PKA) activation and intracellular cyclic AMP ($\text{cAMP}$) generation.
