# DAXDA.IA — Honest Demonstration Walkthrough

**Format:** 42-second narrated concept demonstration

**Disclosure:** This is a visual explanation of the intended prototype workflow, not a claim of a finished enterprise platform or live production deployment.

## Narration
Welcome to DAXDA.IA. This short demonstration shows the intended review workflow. First, an AI-assisted output or decision claim is captured. Next, the reviewer identifies assumptions, evidence gaps, uncertainty, and potential contradictions. DAXDA.IA organizes these challenges into a documented decision record, including risk flags and points requiring human escalation. This is an early-stage, non-production prototype workflow. It is intended to strengthen, not replace, expert judgment.

## Visual sequence
1. Capture the original output or decision claim.
2. Identify assumptions, evidence gaps, uncertainty, and contradictions.
3. Generate a documented decision record with risk flags and escalation points.
4. End with the honest-stage notice: early-stage, non-production, human judgment remains in control.
