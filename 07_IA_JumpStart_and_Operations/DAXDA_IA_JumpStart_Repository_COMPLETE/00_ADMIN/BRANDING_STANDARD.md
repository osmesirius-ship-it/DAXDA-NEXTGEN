# DAXDA.IA Branding Standard

**External product and company name:** `DAXDA.IA`

Use `DAXDA.IA` in all public-facing documents, decks, outreach, filenames, and meetings.

**Founder names for current materials**
- Nicole Bess — Founder and Lead Architect
- Alexander Tyski — Co-Founder / Strategic Partner

**Positioning line**
DAXDA.IA is an early-stage AI-governance and decision-audit framework that helps teams challenge and document AI-assisted outputs before relying on them in consequential workflows.

**Stage language**
Use: framework, prototype workflow, pilot-ready materials, design-partner exploration, deidentified examples, human-in-the-loop review.

Avoid claiming: deployed enterprise software, production security certification, validated performance metrics, customers, revenue, or legal/compliance guarantees unless independently documented.
